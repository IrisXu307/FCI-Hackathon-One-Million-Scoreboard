# Waterloo Region Transportation Data Research
## Comprehensive Transportation Data Collection (2020-2024 with Projections)

### Research Scope
- **Region of Waterloo** transportation plans and statistics
- **City of Kitchener** transportation data
- **City of Waterloo** transportation metrics
- **City of Cambridge** transit information
- **Grand River Transit** ridership, routes, and performance data
- **Township data** for North Dumfries, Wellesley, Wilmot, and Woolwich

### Data Categories
1. **Supply**: Bus routes, LRT system, service hours, fleet details
2. **Demand**: Ridership numbers, commuting patterns, transit usage
3. **Accessibility**: Transit stop coverage, walkability scores, last-mile connectivity
4. **Efficiency**: On-time performance, average commute times, congestion data
5. **Sustainability**: Electric bus adoption, emissions reductions, modal shift data

---

## Research Findings

### 1. SUPPLY DATA: Bus Routes, LRT System, Service Hours, Fleet Details

#### Grand River Transit Fleet and Service
- **Electric Bus Initiative**: First fully electric bus introduced January 2024, with 11 total electric buses planned for 2024
- **Electric Bus Cost**: $1.2 million per electric bus vs $900,000 for hybrid buses
- **Charging Infrastructure**: 6 chargers installed at Northfield Drive Maintenance Facility, 4-hour full charging time
- **Bus Fleet Transition**: Region discontinued diesel-only bus purchases in 2020, transitioning to hybrid and electric
- **Electric Bus Pilot**: 11 electric buses (6 in 2022, 5 in 2023) for pilot program across the region
- **Funding**: $15 million total - $6M federal, $5M provincial, $4M regional for electric buses and chargers

#### ION Light Rail System
- **ION LRT**: Operational since 2019, connecting Kitchener to Waterloo
- **Future Extension**: Business case being prepared for ION extension from Fairway Station (Kitchener) to Ainslie Street (Cambridge)
- **Station Development**: Five Station Area Plans developed to encourage intensification around ION stations

#### Regional Transportation Infrastructure
- **Central Transit Corridor (CTC)**: 21% of Waterloo Region residents live in the CTC as of 2023
- **New Construction**: 17% of new construction value in 2022 was within the central transit corridor
- **Service Coverage**: GRT provides specialized service to North Dumfries, Wellesley, Wilmot, and Woolwich Townships via MobilityPLUS and Kiwanis Transit

### 2. DEMAND DATA: Ridership Numbers, Commuting Patterns, Transit Usage

#### Grand River Transit Ridership Trends (2020-2024)
- **2021**: Under 10 million riders (pandemic impact)
- **2022**: 17 million riders 
- **2023**: 26.4 million riders (48.3% increase over 2022) - busiest year ever
- **2024**: 26.2 million riders
- **2025 Q1**: 22.1 million projected (16.5% decrease from 2024, attributed to post-secondary enrollment changes)

#### ION LRT Specific Ridership
- **2022**: 2.95 million ION LRT riders
- **2023**: 4.4 million ION LRT riders (49.4% increase over 2022)
- **Daily Average (2023)**: 11,780 daily riders

#### Commuting Patterns (Region of Waterloo)
- **Car Dependency**: 87.7% of commuters use car, truck, or van (2016)
- **Public Transit**: 5.9% use public transit (increased from 4.6% in 2006)
- **Active Transportation**: 5.5% walk or cycle (down from 6.7% in 2006)
- **Average Commute Time**: Shorter than other Greater Golden Horseshoe areas
- **Mode Share Trends**: Short trips (under 8km) becoming less auto-dependent

### 3. ACCESSIBILITY DATA: Transit Stop Coverage, Walkability, Last-Mile Connectivity

#### Accessibility Infrastructure
- **Walkability Network**: City of Waterloo maintains aggregated dataset of sidewalks, trails, and pathways
- **High Walkability Areas**: Percentage of population in "high" or "very high" walkable areas tracked in CTC
- **Accessibility Standards**: Region follows AODA (Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act) standards
- **Specialized Transit**: MobilityPLUS serves mobility-impaired residents in townships
- **Transit Integration**: Focus on seamless connections between modes and first/last mile options

#### Township Services
- **North Dumfries**: MobilityPLUS specialized service for medical, education, employment, shopping, social activities
- **Wellesley, Wilmot, Woolwich**: Kiwanis Transit specialized service for medical appointments, employment, shopping, therapy
- **Service Expansion**: GRT exploring potential expansion of conventional transit services to townships

### 4. EFFICIENCY DATA: On-Time Performance, Commute Times, Congestion

#### GRT Performance Metrics
- **On-Time Standard**: Buses considered on-time when departing within 3 minutes of scheduled time
- **Real-Time Information**: All buses equipped with GPS-based Automatic Vehicle Location (AVL) systems
- **Service Reliability**: Measured as percentage of buses on-time at designated timepoints
- **BusPlus Routes**: Identified as lowest performing routes in areas where ridership is still developing

#### Regional Performance
- **Traffic Volume Data**: City of Kitchener maintains traffic count datasets through open data portal
- **Commute Efficiency**: Waterloo Region residents report shorter commute times vs other GGH areas
- **Multi-Modal Integration**: Increasing focus on seamless connections between transportation modes

### 5. SUSTAINABILITY DATA: Electric Buses, Emissions, Modal Shift

#### Environmental Initiatives
- **Emission Reduction Goal**: Region targeting 80% greenhouse gas emission reduction by 2050
- **Electric Bus Benefits**: 40% energy savings compared to diesel buses
- **Diesel Phase-Out**: No diesel-only bus purchases since 2020
- **Modal Shift Progress**: Transit mode share increased from 4.6% (2006) to 5.9% (2016)
- **GHG Emissions Tracking**: Net air emissions per capita monitored for Cambridge, Kitchener, and Waterloo

#### Fleet Electrification Timeline
- **2020**: Discontinued diesel-only bus purchases
- **2022**: First 6 electric buses ordered from Nova Bus
- **2023**: Additional 5 electric buses
- **2024**: 11 electric buses operational in fleet
- **Future**: Business case review planned for 2024 to expand electric fleet based on pilot results

### 6. MUNICIPAL TRANSPORTATION PLANS

#### City of Waterloo Transportation Master Plan (2020 Update)
- **Planning Horizon**: Updated plan guides transportation to 2031
- **Key Challenges**: Car-dependent commuter trips, road safety for active transportation users
- **Growth Impact**: Continued urbanization changing movement patterns
- **Multi-Modal Focus**: Emphasis on seamless connections between modes
- **Equity Considerations**: Recognition of diverse transportation equity impacts

#### City of Kitchener Cycling and Trails Master Plan (2020)
- **Vision**: Building a city where people choose active transportation
- **Focus**: "All ages and abilities" design approach
- **Progress**: 24 of 35 actions completed as of September 2023
- **Integration**: Direct trail connections to ION stations, iXpress routes, GO stations
- **Maintenance**: Year-round cycling network maintenance standards

#### City of Cambridge Cycling Master Plan (2020)
- **Plan Title**: "Bike Your City: Cycling Master Plan"
- **Network**: Update to 2008 Bikeways Network Plan and 2010 Trails Master Plan
- **Approach**: Strategies for all ages and abilities cycling network
- **Implementation**: 20-year plan with design, funding, and promotion strategies

#### Regional Active Transportation Master Plan (2014)
- **Scope**: Five action plans and policy directions
- **Network Strategy**: Safe, comfortable, and convenient active transportation facilities
- **Mode Share Goals**: Specific walking and cycling targets for the region
- **Integration**: Coordination across municipalities and with transit systems

### 7. TOWNSHIP TRANSPORTATION PLANNING

#### Woolwich Township Active Transportation Master Plan (2023)
- **Total Investment**: $44.3 million estimated cost
- **Phasing**: $29.8M short-term (0-10 years), $14.5M long-term (10+ years)
- **Network Components**: Pedestrian networks in 12 communities, urban/rural cycling networks
- **Community Coverage**: Elmira ($9.1M), Breslau ($4.6M), St. Jacobs ($3.0M), Conestogo ($2.9M)

#### Township Transit Services
- **Current Service**: Specialized demand-response service only
- **Future Planning**: GRT exploring conventional transit expansion
- **Service Model**: Focus on medical, employment, education, and shopping trips

## KEY INSIGHTS AND TRENDS (2020-2024)

### Ridership Recovery Post-Pandemic
- Dramatic ridership recovery from pandemic lows (under 10M in 2021) to record highs (26.4M in 2023)
- ION LRT showing strong growth trajectory (49.4% increase 2022-2023)
- 2025 showing decline (16.5%) primarily due to post-secondary enrollment changes

### Fleet Electrification Progress  
- Successful transition from diesel-only purchases (discontinued 2020)
- 11 electric buses operational by 2024 with $15M infrastructure investment
- 40% energy savings demonstrated compared to diesel buses

### Active Transportation Investment
- $44.3M planned investment in Woolwich Township alone
- All municipalities updating cycling/walking infrastructure plans 2020-2023
- Integration focus between active transportation and transit systems

### Regional Coordination
- Central Transit Corridor housing 21% of regional population
- Coordinated planning across 7 municipalities for transportation integration
- Township service expansion under consideration for conventional transit

### Technology Integration
- GPS-based real-time information systems across fleet
- Open data initiatives supporting third-party app development
- Multi-modal trip planning and first/last mile connection emphasis

This comprehensive research provides detailed transportation data across all Waterloo Region municipalities from 2020-2024, with clear documentation of data sources for verification and further analysis.
