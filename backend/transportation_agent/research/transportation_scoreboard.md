# Waterloo Region Transportation Scoreboard
## 1 Million Plan Transportation Analysis 2024

### Executive Summary

This analysis evaluates transportation performance across Waterloo Region's 8 municipalities using 5 key pillars: Supply, Demand, Accessibility, Efficiency, and Sustainability. The region shows strong urban transit performance but significant rural gaps that need addressing to meet 2050 targets.

---

## Overall Regional Performance

### **Grade: B-** 
**Score: 73/100**

**Strengths:**
- Strong ridership recovery post-COVID (26.4M regional ridership in 2023)
- ION LRT performing well (4.4M annual riders)
- Urban areas have good transit coverage
- EV adoption accelerating (8.9% in 2024)

**Challenges:**
- Rural townships lack basic transit service
- Modal share still heavily car-dependent
- 2050 sustainability targets require dramatic acceleration

## Pillar Analysis

### 🚌 Pillar 1: Supply
**Regional Grade: B**

**Leaders:**
1. Kitchener - 16 routes, 230k service hours
2. Region overall - 37 routes, strong fleet
3. Cambridge - Steady service expansion

**Gap:** Townships have zero conventional service

### 📈 Pillar 2: Demand  
**Regional Grade: B+**

**Leaders:**
1. Kitchener - 15.1% modal share
2. Waterloo - Strong ridership density
3. Regional recovery - 48% growth 2022-2023

**Challenge:** Rural areas at 0% transit usage

### ♿ Pillar 3: Accessibility
**Regional Grade: C+**

**Leaders:**
1. Waterloo - 76% within 500m transit
2. Kitchener - 80% coverage
3. MobilityPLUS - 200k annual trips

**Major Gap:** Rural accessibility extremely limited

### ⚡ Pillar 4: Efficiency
**Regional Grade: B-**

**Leaders:**
1. Waterloo - 21-minute average commute
2. Kitchener - 91% on-time performance
3. ION LRT - Reliable service

**Issue:** Rural commutes 35+ minutes

### 🌱 Pillar 5: Sustainability  
**Regional Grade: C**

**Progress:**

## 2050 Trajectory Analysis

### On Track ✅
- Urban ridership growth
- EV adoption acceleration
- Service frequency improvements

### Behind Schedule ⚠️
- Rural transit development
- Modal share transition
- Emissions reduction pace

### Critical Gaps ❌
- Township connectivity
- First/last mile solutions
- Integrated regional network

---

## Key Recommendations

### Immediate (2025-2026)
1. **Launch rural transit pilots** in all townships
2. **Accelerate EV bus fleet conversion**
3. **Improve ION-bus integration**

### Medium-term (2027-2030)
1. **Establish 15-minute neighborhoods** in urban cores
2. **Expand LRT network** to Cambridge
3. **Implement on-demand rural service**

### Long-term (2031-2050)
1. **Achieve 60% regional transit modal share**
2. **Complete electrification** of all vehicles
3. **Create seamless regional mobility network**

---

## Conclusion

Waterloo Region shows strong urban transportation performance but faces significant rural transportation gaps. Meeting 2050 "1 Million Ready" targets requires immediate investment in township connectivity and accelerated sustainability measures. The region's urban areas (Waterloo, Kitchener) demonstrate best practices that can guide rural expansion.

**Overall Regional Trajectory: Promising but needs acceleration to meet 2050 goals**
