## KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

### FEDERAL DATA STRENGTH
✅ **Strong Data Available:**
- Urban greenness measurements (satellite-based, 2000-2025)
- Housing market outlook and rental data
- Population and census demographics (2021)
- Public transit and infrastructure investment ($115M+ 2025)
- Grant program data (FCM tree planting: $4.4M to region)

### GAPS IN FEDERAL DATA
⚠️ **Limited Data:**
- Parks per capita for Cambridge and Waterloo (Kitchener has 7.3 ha/1000)
- Individual municipality housing data (available only aggregated at CMA level)
- Township-specific metrics (minimal federal reporting)
- Household parks access by municipality (available nationally only)

❌ **Not Tracked Federally:**
- Public space quality assessments
- Walkability metrics
- Community facility utilization
- Social amenity access beyond parks
