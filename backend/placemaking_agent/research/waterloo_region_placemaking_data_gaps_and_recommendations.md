# Waterloo Region Placemaking Metrics: Data Gaps and Research Recommendations

**Prepared:** January 2026  
**Focus:** Identifying missing federal data and pathways to obtain comprehensive placemaking metrics

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A comprehensive search of federal data sources reveals significant availability of **macro-level placemaking metrics** for Waterloo Region (particularly through Statistics Canada and Infrastructure Canada), but **limited granular municipal-level data** for smaller communities. This document outlines specific gaps and practical recommendations for obtaining complete placemaking metrics for all seven municipalities.

---

## SECTION 1: DATA AVAILABILITY SUMMARY

### What's Well-Documented (Federal Level):
✅ **Statistics Canada Urban Greenness** - Satellite imagery data (2000-2025) for population centres
✅ **CMHC Housing Market Data** - Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA aggregated quarterly reports
✅ **Infrastructure Canada Funding** - Program announcements and investment tracking
✅ **FCM Grant Programs** - Tree planting and green infrastructure funding
✅ **Census Data** - Population, housing units, density (2021 Census)

### What's Partially Available:
⚠️ **Parks and Green Space Access** - National/provincial data available; municipal breakdowns require data requests
⚠️ **Public Amenities** - Large city-level data (Kitchener, Cambridge, Waterloo); township data scarce
⚠️ **Housing by Municipality** - Aggregated at CMA level in CMHC reports

### What's Missing or Limited:
❌ **Per Capita Metrics for Smaller Municipalities** - North Dumfries, Wellesley, Wilmot, Woolwich have minimal federal reporting
❌ **Household-Level Parks Access** - Statistics Canada data available nationally but not disaggregated by Waterloo Region municipality
❌ **Historical Trend Data (2020-2022)** - Often published only after 2-3 year lag
❌ **Public Space Investment Data** - Infrastructure Canada announces projects but does not maintain consolidated regional database
❌ **Walkability/Pedestrian Infrastructure Metrics** - Not systematically collected at federal level
❌ **Community Space Utilization Data** - Facility usage statistics not captured in federal datasets

## SECTION 3: FEDERAL DATA REQUEST PROCEDURES

### Statistics Canada Data Requests

**What You Can Request:**
1. **Table 38-10-0022-01 (Parks and Green Spaces)**
   - Household-level access data disaggregated by Waterloo Region municipality
   - Historical years (2015-2024)
   - By income quintiles

2. **Table 38-10-0158-01 (Urban Greenness)**
   - Specific NDVI values for each municipality
   - Pixel-level detail available for mapping
   - Historical annual data (2000-2025)

3. **Custom Tabulation**
   - 2021 Census data for specific geographic breakdowns
   - Housing characteristics by municipality
   - Specific cross-tabulations not in standard release tables

**How to Request:**
- **Email:** statcan.infostats-infostats.statcan@canada.ca
- **Phone:** 1-800-263-1136
- **Web Form:** https://www.statcan.gc.ca/eng/about/service/statcan-customer-service-centers
- **Cost:** $0-$500+ depending on complexity and file format
- **Timeline:** 2-8 weeks typical turnaround
- **Special Request Portal:** https://www.statcan.gc.ca/eng/about/service/custom-tabulation-service

## SECTION 9: NOTABLE DATA DELAYS AND LIMITATIONS

### 2020-2022 Data Gaps
**Issue:** COVID-19 disrupted data collection and publication
- Statistics Canada delayed Census results (released 2021-2022)
- CMHC market outlooks based on limited 2020-2021 data
- Infrastructure projects paused; reporting delayed
- Park usage data limited for 2020-2021 period

**Recommendation:** Use 2023-2025 data as baseline for recent trends; acknowledge 2020-2021 limitations

### Aggregation at CMA Level
**Issue:** CMHC reports Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo as single CMA
- Individual city housing data not available in federal reports
- Must request municipal-level detail separately
- Comparison with peer cities difficult

**Recommendation:** Request CMHC municipal-level detail; supplement with local housing authority data

### Township Data Limitations
**Issue:** Federal surveys often focus on large urban centers
- Statistics Canada samples may undercount small municipality detail
- Minimum population thresholds for reporting
- Limited federal infrastructure investment tracking

**Recommendation:** Rely on local government data for townships; supplement with regional aggregate data

### Parks Data Collection Methods
**Issue:** No standardized federal parks inventory
- Statistics Canada focuses on greenness (satellite imagery)
- Canadian City Parks Report uses varied municipal methods
- Definition of "parks" differs by municipality

**Recommendation:** Request municipal park inventories; standardize definitions before comparison

## CONCLUSION

**Federal data availability for Waterloo Region placemaking is:
- **Strong** for macro-level metrics (greenness, housing, infrastructure investment)
- **Moderate** for municipal-level data (park provision, public amenities)
- **Limited** for township-level detail (North Dumfries, Wellesley, Wilmot, Woolwich)

**Strategic approach:**
1. Leverage strong federal data for aggregate placemaking metrics
2. Pursue formal data requests for municipal-level breakdowns
3. Supplement with local government data for detailed placemaking assessment
4. Establish ongoing relationships with data stewards for regular updates
