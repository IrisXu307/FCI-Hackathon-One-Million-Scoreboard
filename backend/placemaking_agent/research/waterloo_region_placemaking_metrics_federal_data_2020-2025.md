## 1. STATISTICS CANADA DATA

### 1.1 Urban Greenness (Census of Environment)

**Data Source:** Statistics Canada - Census of Environment Program
**Table:** 38-10-0158-01 (Urban greenness and normalized difference vegetation index by 2021 population centre)
**Years Available:** 2000-2025 (annual data)

#### Key Statistics:
- **Measurement Method:** Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) derived from satellite imagery
- **Definition:** Percentage of land area classified as green (NDVI ≥ 0.5)

#### Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA Status:
- **Kitchener Performance (2021-2022):** Maintaining/increasing greenness
- **Ontario Trend (Large Urban Centres):** Decreased 10.5 percentage points from 2000-2004 baseline to 2018-2022 period
- **2025 Update:** More than 68.5% of large urban population centres classified as green (down from 70.3% in 2024)
- **Status:** Kitchener and Guelph are exceptions in Ontario - both show increasing urban greenness while most cities are losing green space

**URL:** https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3810015801
**Release:** Annual (most recent November 2025)
**Publication:** The Daily — Census of Environment: Urban greenness, 2022 & 2025
https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/221117/dq221117e-eng.htm

## 2. CANADIAN MORTGAGE AND HOUSING CORPORATION (CMHC) DATA

### 2.1 Housing Market Outlook - Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA

**2025 Outlook (February 2025):**
- **Home Price Forecast 2025:** Approx. $788,750 average (down from $791,691 in 2024)
- **Housing Trend:** Notable rise of housing construction expected in 2025
- **Driver:** Primarily multi-unit construction and rental housing development
- **Rental Market:** Low vacancy expected with new supply additions
- **Migration Trend:** Increased buyer interest from Greater Toronto Area (GTA)
- **International Student Impact:** Fewer international students could increase rental vacancy

**2024 Housing Market Outlook:**
- Kitchener, Cambridge, and Waterloo grouped together in CMHC reports
- Rental housing demand remains strong
- Purpose-built rental apartments continuing to expand
- Market conditions softer in 2024-2025 period
