1. Waterloo's Walkability Excellence
   - City of Waterloo #2 in Ontario (2024)
   - 47,710 weekly walking trips
   - Demonstrates successful placemaking policy implementation
   
2. Major Transit Investment Confirmed
   - $291 million federal-provincial-regional commitment (Dec 2025)
   - $131 million Kitchener Central Transit Hub (placemaking focus)
   - Construction starting 2025
   
3. Vision 1 Million Framework Established
   - First Ontario framework treating placemaking equal to housing/transit
   - Version 1 released January 2026
   - Comprehensive 5-domain assessment
   
4. Significant Growth Projected
   - Kitchener: +127,720 residents by 2051 (+40%)
   - 96,353 housing units needed
   - 54,100 new jobs projected
   
5. Planning Authority Restructuring
   - Bill 23 implementation (Jan 1, 2025)
   - Authority shifted to 7 area municipalities
   - Challenge for cross-municipal consistency
