# Waterloo Region Placemaking Research Compilation
**Regional and Municipal Data on Placemaking, Parks, Public Spaces, and Community Infrastructure (2020-2025)**

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document compiles comprehensive regional and municipal data on placemaking initiatives across Waterloo Region, including the Region of Waterloo, City of Kitchener, City of Waterloo, and City of Cambridge. The research covers official plans, strategic initiatives, parks and public spaces, transit accessibility, municipal statistics, and implementation updates on the "1 Million Plan" (Vision 1 Million Scorecard).

## 3. TRANSIT AND ACCESSIBILITY

### Grand River Transit (GRT) Performance & Accessibility

#### GRT Ridership Update - 2024 Year End

**Document:** Grand River Transit – Ridership Update (Report TSD-TRS-25-004)
**Date:** March 4, 2025
**Source:** https://pub-regionofwaterloo.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11628

**2024 Key Metrics:**
- Service hours increased by **10%**
- On-time performance improved by **8%**
- Slight decline in overall ridership compared to 2023 (record year)
- Service enhancements and network improvements planned for September 2024

**Historical Ridership Trends:**
- Grown by at least 9% since 2015 (from 20.3 million trips)
- At least 1% growth since 2019 (from 22 million trips)
- 2023: Record-breaking year

**Q1 2025 Update (March 2025):**
- Ridership decline of 16.5% compared to Q1 2024
- Primary factor: Fluctuations in post-secondary student populations
- Service reevaluation based on current demand patterns

**2025 Ridership Decline (March 2025):**
- **4.1 million fewer riders** compared to 2024
- Attributed to international student cap policy
- MobilityPLUS (specialized transit) saw **16% increase** (199,600 trips)
- Route 302 ION bus service matched to weekday ION LRT for connections

### ION Light Rail Transit

#### ION Stage 1 Impact Data

**Central Transit Corridor (CTC) 2024 Monitoring Report**

**Source:** https://www.regionofwaterloo.ca/en/regional-government/resources/LandUse_BuildingActivity/Monitoring_Change_in_the_CTC_2024_Report.pdf
**Date:** 2024

**CTC Coverage:** Connects Cambridge, Kitchener, and Waterloo (downtown cores)

**Monitoring Goals & Indicators:**

**MOVING PEOPLE:**
- Mobility: Number of GRT trips (millions)
- Daily Transit Activity: % of daily average transit activity in CTC
- Sustainable Modes: Transit mode share (%)
- Active Transportation: Pedestrian and cyclist mode share (%)
- GHG Emissions: Tonnes of net air emissions per capita
- Walkability: % of population in 'high'/'very high' walkable areas

**BUILDING COMMUNITY:**
- Land Use Mix: % of regional land uses in CTC
- Population: % of Region's residents in CTC
- MTSA Density: Density at each MTSA
- Arts & Culture: Number of arts/culture establishments
- Restaurants: % of Region's restaurants in CTC
- Heritage: Demolition permits on pre-1920/designated resources
- Investment: Dollar value of building permits for new construction
- Assessment Value: Assessed value of properties in CTC

**INCLUSIVE COMMUNITY:**
- Affordability: % of housing transactions affordable to low/moderate-income households
- Community Housing: Number of units in CTC
- Crime & Safety: Perception of safety in downtowns
- Calls for Service: % of police calls related to public perception

**Key Community Housing Finding:**
- 3,077 Community Housing units in CTC (as of recent count)
- 330 more units than 2011 = **12% increase**
- Continued success of ION accomplishing "moving people" and "building community" goals

**Data Status Note:**
- Some indicators (marked with *) not completed for 2023 due to data/resource limitations
- Includes: Cultural Vibrancy, Transit Mode Share, Active Transportation, Perception of Safety

**Source Report:** https://kitchener.citynews.ca/2024/06/19/region-unveils-preferred-design-for-victoria-street-redesign-for-future-transit-hub/

- Public-facing tool grounded in data
- Track progress across key areas

**Five Key Readiness Areas:**
1. **Housing Supply** - Currently NOT on track to meet needs
2. **Transportation Infrastructure** - Currently NOT on track to meet needs
3. **Healthcare Services** - Currently NOT on track to meet needs
4. **Employment Opportunities** - On track to meet needs ✓
5. **Placemaking and Livability** - Current status under evaluation

**Assessment Summary:**
> "Currently, our housing supply, transportation infrastructure, healthcare services, employment opportunities, and social infrastructure is not advancing at the pace needed overall to support this expansion." - BestWR

**Areas On Track:**
- Affordable housing initiatives (ongoing municipal push)
- Regional employment levels (stronger)

**First Review Cycle:**
- FCI confirming baseline data
- Aligning metrics with Waterloo Region initiatives
- Quarterly or annual updates planned

**University Partnership:**
- Future Cities Institute (FCI) - founded by CAIVAN at University of Waterloo
- Collaboration with BestWR for data confirmation and alignment
