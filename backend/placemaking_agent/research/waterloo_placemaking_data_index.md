### KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS TRACKED

#### Moving People Goals:
- Transit ridership (millions of trips)
- Daily transit activity in CTC
- Transit mode share (% of travel)
- Active transportation share (walking/cycling %)
- GHG emissions per capita
- Walkability (% in high/very high walkable areas)

#### Building Community Goals:
- Land use mix in CTC
- Regional population in CTC
- Arts & culture establishments
- Restaurant presence
- Heritage resource retention (demolition permits)
- Investment activity (building permits $ value)
- Property assessment values

#### Inclusive Community Goals:
- Affordable housing transactions
- Community housing units
- Perception of downtown safety
- Police calls related to public perception

### RESEARCH DATA QUALITY ASSESSMENT

**Strong/Complete Data:**
- ✅ Transit ridership (best time series)
- ✅ Capital project inventories
- ✅ Strategic plan documentation
- ✅ Housing statistics (counting ongoing)
- ✅ Parks acreage and facilities
- ✅ Walking/cycling rankings (MOT data)

**Moderate Data:**
- ⚠️ CTC monitoring indicators (2023 gaps noted)
- ⚠️ Cultural venue counts (tracking improving)
- ⚠️ Public amenity inventory (location-specific)
- ⚠️ Safety perception (not fully completed)
- ⚠️ Event attendance (selective reporting)

**Gaps Identified:**
- ❌ Real-time foot traffic by location
- ❌ Disaggregated crime data by downtown area
- ❌ Complete accessibility audit results
- ❌ Operational funding (detailed breakdown)
- ❌ Community programming attendance by type
- ❌ Dwell time analytics
- ❌ Specific placemaking ROI metrics

## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY NOTES

**Search Strategy:**
- Targeted searches for each municipality + region
- Specific queries for parks, transit, housing, walkability
- Strategic plan and official plan document review
- News and media coverage (2023-2025)
- Official municipal reports and statistics

**Time Period:** 2020-2025 (emphasis on 2023-2025)

**Compilation Method:** Systematic extraction from 54+ primary sources
