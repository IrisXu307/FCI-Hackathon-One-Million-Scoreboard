### B. RESEARCH FINDINGS ON PLACEMAKING

#### From "Recent History of Urban Placemaking in Kitchener" Study
**Key Themes Identified**:

1. **Rise of Placemaking**: Deliberate, strategic place-based development in downtown Kitchener
   - Heritage Conservation intersection with innovation
   - Developer commitment to quality placemaking ("space has to feel good" - Craig Beattie)

2. **Heritage & Innovation Integration**: 
   - Adaptive reuse of industrial buildings (Tannery, Rumpel Felt Factory, Kaufman Lofts)
   - Preservation as part of innovation district strategy
   - Key projects: Breithaupt Block, Simpson Block, THEMUSEUM expansion

3. **Youthification**: 
   - Downtown attracting younger demographic
   - "Cool and trendy" urban change
   - Proximity effects (location attractiveness)

4. **Innovation District Development**: 
   - Location-based economic strategy
   - Cluster building (tech, creative industries)
   - Mixed-use development patterns

---

#### From Dr. Troy Glover's Transformative Placemaking Research
**Core Concepts**:
- **Social Connectedness**: Placemaking creates conditions for meaningful connections
- **Community Change**: Positive outcomes for people and communities through place-based work
- **Public Realm Shaping**: Intentional design of spaces to facilitate interaction
- **Measurable Impacts**: Community well-being outcomes linked to placemaking

---

#### From Future Cities Institute Vision 1 Million Research
**Placemaking & Livability Domain Findings**:
- Framework for measuring placemaking readiness
- Links to housing, transportation, employment
- Baseline data establishing regional scorecard
- System-level view of interconnected urban systems

## PART 4: RECOMMENDED METRICS DASHBOARD

### Tier 1: Currently Available & Reliable

**[RECOMMENDED FOR IMMEDIATE USE]**

1. **Community Well-being Index** (Vital Signs 2025)
   - Life satisfaction (49%)
   - Mental health trends
   - Social infrastructure access
   - *Update frequency*: Annual

2. **Economic Development Scorecard** (EDC + WREDS)
   - Investment targets and pipeline
   - Sector growth rates
   - Job creation trends
   - *Update frequency*: Annual

3. **Real Estate Market Health** (Cornerstone REALTORS)
   - Home sales volume
   - Price indices
   - Market trends by area
   - *Update frequency*: Monthly

4. **Population & Growth Metrics** (Region + Statistics Canada)
   - Population estimates
   - Demographic composition
   - Growth rate tracking
   - *Update frequency*: Annual (census every 5 years)

5. **Transit & Infrastructure Impact** (CTC Monitoring)
   - Housing development in corridor
   - Investment tracking
   - Ridership trends
   - *Update frequency*: Annual

6. **Arts & Culture Investment** (Municipal sources)
   - Annual funding
   - Event participation (where available)
   - Grant distribution
   - *Update frequency*: Annual

---

### Tier 2: Available but Incomplete

**[RECOMMENDED FOR SUPPLEMENTARY USE]**

1. **Business District Health** (BIA Reports)
   - Asset conditions
   - Business collaboration initiatives
   - Strategic planning
   - *Gap*: Limited quantitative metrics
   - *Update frequency*: Annual to 3-year cycle

2. **Community Engagement** (Municipal platforms)
   - Engagement participation rates
   - Strategic plan alignment
   - Resident input collected
   - *Gap*: Outcome tracking
   - *Update frequency*: Ongoing/Annual

3. **Downtown Revitalization Progress** (Project-specific)
   - Heritage adaptations
   - New development completions
   - Occupancy rates
   - *Gap*: Aggregated metrics
   - *Update frequency*: Project-dependent

---

### Tier 3: Needed for Complete Picture

**[RECOMMENDED FOR FUTURE DEVELOPMENT]**

1. **Real-time Foot Traffic & Activity**
   - Neighborhood vibrancy index
   - Peak activity times
   - Pedestrian counts by location
   - *Feasibility*: Medium (sensor networks, mobile data)
   - *Cost*: Moderate
   - *Value*: High

2. **Public Space Usage Analytics**
   - Park utilization rates
   - Plaza activity patterns
   - Event attendance tracking
   - *Feasibility*: Medium (observation, sensors)
   - *Cost*: Moderate
   - *Value*: High

3. **Retail/Commercial Health Index**
   - Occupancy rates by district
   - Business turnover rates
   - Lease price trends
   - *Feasibility*: Medium (commercial real estate partnership)
   - *Cost*: Low-Moderate
   - *Value*: Very High

4. **Neighborhood-level Social Cohesion**
   - Geographic Vital Signs breakdown
   - Local event participation
   - Neighbor connectedness measures
   - *Feasibility*: Medium (targeted surveys)
   - *Cost*: Moderate-High
   - *Value*: High
