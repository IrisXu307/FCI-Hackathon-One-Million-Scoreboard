## KEY METRICS & DATA POINTS BY CATEGORY

### A. COMMUNITY WELL-BEING & SOCIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

**Primary Source**: Waterloo Region Vital Signs® 2025 Report
- Life Satisfaction: **49% report high satisfaction**
- Mental Health Decline: **More severe in Waterloo Region than national average**
- Growth-Satisfaction Gap: **Rapid population growth not matched by social infrastructure**
- Social Infrastructure Focus: Parks, community centers, libraries, cultural institutions, programming

**Secondary Source**: Food Bank of Waterloo Region 2025
- Food Assistance Visits: **622,000+ visits**
- Trend: Growing long-term reliance (affordability indicator)

---

### B. ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT & INVESTMENT

**Primary Source**: Waterloo EDC 2024 Annual Report
- Municipal Funding: $2.8M
- Federal Funding: $1.18M
- Provincial Funding: $442K
- Total Revenue: $4.5M
- Investment Goal (2024-2028): Close $2.4B in deals, maintain $1B pipeline

**Source**: Region of Waterloo Economic Indicators
- GDP: **>2% annual growth, Top 5 metropolitan areas in Canada**
- Job Growth: **Robust and steady over 15+ years**
- Building Permits: **Strong growth, concentrated on Central Transit Corridor**

---

### C. REAL ESTATE & HOUSING MARKET (2025)

**Primary Source**: Cornerstone REALTORS - MLS Data
- Total Sales (2025): **6,177 homes**
- Year-over-Year Change: **-8.8% (vs 2024)**
- 10-Year Average: **-25.3% (vs long-term baseline)**
- Kitchener-Waterloo HPI: **-9.3% YoY (price decline slowing)**
- Cambridge: **Stabilizing with slight uptick**

**Interpretation**: Market cooling but stabilizing; potential floor formation

---

### D. TRANSIT & PLACEMAKING INFRASTRUCTURE (ION LRT)

**Primary Source**: Monitoring Change in Central Transit Corridor Report
- Community Housing Units (CTC): **3,077 units (12% increase since 2011)**
- Investment Metrics: **Tracking positive development patterns**
- Dual Goals Assessment: **Moving people & building community - exceeding expectations**

**Recent Investment** (2025):
- Total: **$291+ million**
- Focus: Kitchener Central Transit Hub, hybrid buses, facility upgrades, accessibility

**Project Cost**: $818 million (Stage 1: Kitchener-Waterloo; Stage 2: Cambridge expansion under consideration)

---

### E. POPULATION & DEMOGRAPHICS

**Base Numbers** (Region of Waterloo):
- Current Population (End 2024): **678,170**
- 2024 Mid-Year Estimate (Stats Canada): **706,000**
- 2021 Census: **614,000**
- Projection by 2051: **923,000**
- Annual Growth Rate: **~1.5% average (past 15 years)**

**Age & Family Structure** (2021 Census):
- One-person households: **26%**
- Couples without children: **24%**
- Couples with children: **28%**
- Never married: **35%** (age 15+)
- Currently married: **46%** (age 15+)

**Children & Youth** (Ages 0-18):
- Total: **128,955**
- Two-parent families: **81.8%**
- One-parent families: **18.1%**
- Racialized population: **33.6%**
  - South Asian: 10.1%
  - Black: 7.8%
  - Arab: 3.0%
