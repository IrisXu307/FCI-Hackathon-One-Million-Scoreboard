# Waterloo Region Housing Scoreboard 2024-2050

## Executive Summary

Waterloo Region is on track to become a 1-million-person region by 2050, requiring unprecedented housing growth. Current data shows strong population expansion (4.5% growth in 2024) driven by international migration, but housing supply has stalled in 2024 with starts plunging to 2,800 units—the lowest in five years. This analysis evaluates housing capacity, affordability, and stability across 5 pillars and 8 geographic areas.
