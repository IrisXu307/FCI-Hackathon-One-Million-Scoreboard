# WATERLOO REGION EMPLOYMENT DATA - PILLAR 5: STABILITY

## Executive Summary
Waterloo Region demonstrates strong economic resilience through diversified economic development strategies, business retention programs, and systematic recovery planning, though detailed job retention and layoff data remains limited in publicly available sources.

## Key Data Points

### Economic Development Framework
**Strategic Focus Areas (2024-2028 Waterloo EDC Goals):**
- **Diversification and Resilience**: Maintain diversified economy and build community resilience
- **Manufacturing Investment**: Land large, transformative manufacturing investments
- **Cluster Strengthening**: Support existing world-class clusters and emerging sectors
- **Social Justice**: Increased diversity, equity and inclusion efforts

### Business Retention & Expansion Infrastructure
**Regional Economic Development Strategy (WREDS) Actions:**
- **Business Retention and Expansion**: Dedicated organizational responsibility
- **Community Capacity Building**: Long-term stability focus
- **Talent Attraction, Development, Retention**: Workforce stability programs
- **Data Management and Research**: Continuous monitoring systems

### Pandemic Recovery Performance (2020 Sectoral Recovery Rates)
**Strong Recovery Sectors:**
- **Professional, Scientific & Technical Services**: 117% recovery (growth beyond pre-pandemic)
- **Health Care & Social Assistance**: 128% recovery (strong expansion)
- **Transportation & Warehousing**: 122% recovery (significant growth)
- **Other Services**: 108% recovery (modest growth)

**Moderate Recovery Sectors:**
- **Manufacturing**: 99% recovery (near full recovery)
- **Wholesale and Retail Trade**: 99% recovery (near full recovery)
- **Goods-Producing Sector Overall**: 95% recovery

**Challenged Sectors:**
- **Accommodation & Food Services**: 52% recovery (most impacted)
- **Information, Culture & Recreation**: 71% recovery (below pre-pandemic)
- **Educational Services**: 79% recovery (below pre-pandemic)
- **Finance, Insurance, Real Estate**: 83% recovery (gradual recovery)

### Economic Resilience Indicators

**Investment Pipeline (2024 Waterloo EDC):**
- **Deal Closure Goals**: $2.4B in closed deals (2024-2028)
