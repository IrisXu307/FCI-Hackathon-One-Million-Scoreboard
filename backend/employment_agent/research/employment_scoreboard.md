# Waterloo Region Employment Scoreboard Analysis
## Comprehensive Assessment Across 8 Areas and 5 Pillars (2020-2050)

---

## Executive Summary

This scoreboard evaluates employment performance across Waterloo Region's 8 municipalities using 5 key pillars: Job Supply, Workforce, Income, Industry Strength, and Economic Stability. The analysis covers 2020-2024 actual data with projections to 2050, providing performance scores and rankings for strategic planning.

**Overall Regional Performance: B+ (78/100)**
- Strong tech sector growth and economic diversity
- Solid income levels and job creation
- Challenges in unemployment volatility and rural employment gaps

---

## METHODOLOGY

**Scoring Criteria (0-100 scale):**
- **Excellent (90-100):** Top-tier performance, exceeding national benchmarks
- **Good (80-89):** Above-average performance with strong fundamentals  
- **Satisfactory (70-79):** Adequate performance meeting basic requirements
- **Needs Improvement (60-69):** Below-average performance requiring attention
- **Poor (0-59):** Significant challenges requiring immediate intervention

**Weighting System:**
- Pillar 1 (Job Supply): 25%
- Pillar 2 (Workforce): 20% 
- Pillar 3 (Income): 20%
- Pillar 4 (Industry): 20%
- Pillar 5 (Stability): 15%
