# WATERLOO REGION EMPLOYMENT DATA - PILLAR 1: JOB SUPPLY

## Executive Summary
Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA experienced the fastest pace of job creation in Ontario in 2024 at 6.5%, particularly in construction and professional, scientific and technical services.

## Key Data Points

### Total Employment Numbers
- **2020**: 306,200 employed people in KCW CMA (Region of Waterloo data)
- **2024**: Significant growth with 6.5% job creation rate (fastest in Ontario)

### Job Growth Performance 
- **2024 Annual Job Growth**: 6.5% (Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA)
- **Comparison**: Above provincial average (Ontario: 1.7% overall)
- **Ranking**: #1 among 15 Ontario CMAs for job creation pace

### Job Creation Rate by Sector (2024)
- **Construction**: Major growth contributor
- **Professional, Scientific & Technical Services**: Major growth contributor  
- **Manufacturing**: Some challenges (-5.8k jobs provincially)
- **Wholesale & Retail Trade**: Challenges (-11.4k job vacancies provincially)

### Labour Force Characteristics
- **2020 Labour Force**: 338,600 people in KCW CMA
- **2020 Population 15+**: 492,900 in KCW CMA
- **Labour Force Participation Rate**: Higher than provincial and national averages

### Workplace Distribution (2024 Regional Data)
- **Total Workplaces**: Data shows 340 industrial, 940 retail, 390 office, 360 institutional workplaces
- **Jobs by Industry Type**: 
  - Industrial: 37,240 jobs
  - Retail: 16,330 jobs  
  - Office: Not specified in initial data
  - Institutional: Not specified in initial data

## Geographic Coverage
