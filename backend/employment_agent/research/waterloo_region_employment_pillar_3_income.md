# WATERLOO REGION EMPLOYMENT DATA - PILLAR 3: INCOME

## Executive Summary
Waterloo Region maintains median household incomes above provincial and national averages, though income inequality challenges persist with significant portions of households in lower income brackets.

## Key Data Points

### Median Household Income
- **2015**: $77,500 (baseline)
- **2020**: $92,000 (18.7% increase from 2015)
  - **Comparison**: Higher than Ontario ($91,000) and Canada ($84,000)
- **Note**: Region consistently ranks among highest incomes in Ontario

### Income Distribution by Household (2021 CMHC Data)
**Kitchener-Cambridge-Waterloo CMA (219,060 total households)**
- **Less than $20,000**: 8,885 households (4.1%)
- **$20,000 to $39,999**: 24,905 households (11.4%)
- **$40,000 to $59,999**: 30,050 households (13.7%)
- **$60,000 to $79,999**: 29,235 households (13.3%)
- **$80,000 to $99,999**: 26,550 households (12.1%)
- **$100,000 and over**: 99,430 households (45.4%)

### Low Income Trends
- **2015**: 22.2% of households living on less than $40,000/year
- **2020**: 15.5% of households living on less than $40,000/year
- **Improvement**: 6.7 percentage point decrease in low-income households

### Wage Data (Job Bank - Kitchener-Waterloo-Barrie Region, 2023-2024)
**Selected Occupations (Low/Median/High hourly wages)**
- **Accounting and related clerks**: $19.00 / $25.82 / $36.00
- **Construction estimators**: $29.23 / $36.06 / $61.54
- **Survey interviewers and statistical clerks**: $17.60 / $26.37 / $36.05

### Income Inequality Indicators
- **Lower Income Concentration**: 15.5% of households below $40,000 (2020)
- **Higher Income Concentration**: 45.4% of households above $100,000
- **Geographic Variation**: Income distribution varies by municipal area within CMA

### Municipal Income Distribution (2021 CMHC)
**Cambridge** (51,270 households):
- Lower income (<$40K): 7,665 households (14.9%)
- Higher income (>$100K): 23,450 households (45.7%)

**Waterloo** (47,040 households):
- Lower income (<$40K): 8,180 households (17.4%)
- Higher income (>$100K): 21,975 households (46.7%)

**Kitchener East** (66,010 households):
- Lower income (<$40K): 8,850 households (13.4%)
- Higher income (>$100K): 29,905 households (45.3%)

## Housing Affordability Context
- **Housing Cost Burden**: Residents spending >30% of income on housing report:
  - 2x more likely to have low life satisfaction
  - 1.5x more likely to experience related challenges

## Regional Wage Trends
- **2022 National Context**: Median wages declined 1.6% after inflation adjustment
- **Regional Performance**: Waterloo Region maintains above-average income levels despite national challenges

## Key Insights
1. **Above-Average Performance**: Region consistently outperforms provincial/national income benchmarks
2. **Improving Low-Income Rates**: Significant reduction in households with income <$40K
3. **Income Polarization**: Strong concentration in both lower and higher income brackets
4. **Geographic Variation**: Income distribution varies across municipalities within the region
5. **Housing Pressure**: Income levels intersect with housing affordability challenges
