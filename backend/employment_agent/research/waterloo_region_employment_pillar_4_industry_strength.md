# WATERLOO REGION EMPLOYMENT DATA - PILLAR 4: INDUSTRY STRENGTH

## Executive Summary
Waterloo Region demonstrates strong industrial diversification with significant strengths in technology, manufacturing, and advanced services, positioning it as a resilient economic hub with sector diversity.

## Key Data Points

### Overall Sector Distribution (2024 Workplace Count)
**Top 10 Sectors by Employment Share:**
1. **Educational Services** (61): Largest employment sector
2. **Retail Trade** (4445): Major retail presence  
3. **Professional, Scientific & Technical Services** (54): High-value services
4. **Manufacturing** (3133): Strong manufacturing base
5. **Health Care & Social Assistance** (62): Essential services
6. **Accommodation & Food Services** (72): Service sector
7. **Finance & Insurance** (52): Financial services
8. **Administrative & Support Services** (56): Business support
9. **Other Services** (81): Diverse service provision
10. **Construction** (23): Infrastructure development

### Technology Sector Performance
- **Tech Job Growth (2018-2023)**: 46% increase
- **North American Ranking**: #3 fastest-growing tech market
- **Tech Talent Concentration**: 9.6% (among top North American markets)
- **ICT Companies**: 1,570 companies with 11,160+ employees (pre-2024 data)
- **Tech Ecosystem Ranking**: Toronto-Waterloo corridor ranked 16th globally (2017)

### Manufacturing Sector Strength
- **National Ranking**: 4th largest manufacturing sector in Canada
- **Employment**: 57,000 people (18.6% of labour force) - 2020 data
- **Companies**: 1,850 manufacturing companies
- **Annual Revenue**: Exceeding $6 billion
- **Automotive Manufacturing**: Significant hiring requirements (22,066 total hiring 2016-2025)

### Aerospace Industry
- **National Ranking**: 4th largest in Canada by company count
- **Employment Ranking**: 3rd largest in Canada by employment
- **Companies**: 100+ aerospace companies
- **Employment**: 3,000+ employed in aerospace

### Key Specialized Sectors

**Health Technology & MedTech:**
- Leading companies: AGFA HealthCare, Canon Medical, Spectramedx, NDI
- Canada's first industry-led medtech hub
- Strong innovation support ecosystem

**Food & Beverage Manufacturing:**
- Regional strength in Kitchener-Waterloo-Barrie area
- 16,283 employed (13.7% of Ontario's food/beverage manufacturing)
- Diverse subsector mix including dairy, meat products, beverages

**Financial Services:**
- Strong fintech presence
- Integration with broader tech ecosystem
- Growing financial technology sector

### Innovation Infrastructure
- **Research & Innovation Centers**: Supporting manufacturing, IT, automotive, health tech
- **Quantum Technology**: Significant regional strength

- **Cybersecurity**: Specialized tech sector strength

### Sector Diversification Benefits
- **Manufacturing**: Traditional strength maintaining employment
- **Technology**: High-growth sector driving innovation
- **Services**: Diverse professional and business services
